About AnonThread

AnonThread is a lightweight, open-source experiment in anonymous, ephemeral discussion. It is built to be minimal, fast, and transparent about how it works — no accounts, no profiles, no permanence.

We are a small group of designers and builders who value privacy, clarity, and community-led software. We design the interface to feel like a terminal transmission: stark, focused, and free of distraction. The goal is simple: let ideas move, then let them fade.

Mission

Give people a place to share ideas without identity baggage. We believe the best conversations happen when the message matters more than the profile, and when software stays out of the way.

We designed the interface to feel like a terminal transmission: stark, focused, and free of distraction. The goal is simple: let ideas move, then let them fade.

Open-source ethos

  • Readable frontend code with zero build tooling required.
  • Supabase-backed persistence that can be swapped or self-hosted.
  • Privacy-forward design: no trackers, no hidden analytics.

PR rules

  • Keep the aesthetic minimal and consistent with the terminal-inspired UI.
  • Prefer small, focused PRs with clear descriptions and reproduction steps for bugs.
  • Document any new UI elements and include screenshots for visual changes.
  • Avoid heavy dependencies or build steps unless absolutely necessary.

How to contribute

Fork the project, propose improvements, and keep the aesthetic intact. If you are adding features, aim for clarity and restraint. If you are fixing bugs, include a concise reproduction note.
